# AVAN Mindset & Affirmations — Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: August 2026*

AVAN is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This document describes the data handling practices for the AVAN mobile application on Google Play.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Processing Principles
- **100% On-Device Personalization**: Survey inputs (goals, psychological archetypes, mood history) are evaluated strictly locally on the user's device using on-device 16-dimensional embedding vectors.
- **Audio & Microphone Permissions**:
  - Permission: `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`
  - Purpose: Enables user voice affirmation playback (*My Voice Studio*) and interactive pronunciation repetition feedback (*Say After Me*).
  - Processing: Audio recordings and speech recognition are processed directly in the local app sandbox. Voice files are never uploaded, analyzed for advertising, or sold to third parties.
- **Journal & Vision Board Data**: Journal thoughts, affirmations, favorites, and visual goal boards remain stored in the device's local sandboxed key-value store.

---

### 2. Payments & Subscriptions
In-app subscriptions (*Monthly* and *Annual*) are processed securely via **Google Play Billing**. AVAN does not collect or store credit card information or billing addresses.

---

### 3. Data Deletion
Users can erase all stored data, journal entries, recordings, and personalization vectors at any time via the **Settings -> Reset App Data** button inside the Profile Tab.

---

### 4. Support Contact
For inquiries regarding data privacy: `support@avanapp.com`
